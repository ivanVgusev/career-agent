# Interview review rubric

Score each category from 0 to 20 and support the score with excerpts from the transcript.

## Relevance

The answer addresses the question and avoids unrelated detail.

## Evidence

The candidate uses concrete examples, names their own contribution and separates facts from assumptions.

## Structure

The listener can follow the situation, action and result without reconstructing the answer.

## Technical accuracy

Terms, architecture and tradeoffs match the candidate's stated experience.

## Communication

The answer is concise, direct and understandable to the intended interviewer.

Record uncertain claims and follow-up questions separately from the numeric score.
